# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Everyone participating in this project — issues, pull requests, discussions,
and reviews — is expected to be civil and constructive. Criticism belongs on
the code, not on the person who wrote it.

Behavior that is not welcome here:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or demeaning comments about anyone, including on the basis of
  age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality,
  personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.
- Publishing others' private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption: bad-faith argument, deliberate derailing, or repeatedly
  reopening settled decisions.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, and when representing the project in public
elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer at
<matthew.digman@vanderbilt.edu>. Reports are handled privately, and the
reporter's identity is not disclosed without their agreement.

The maintainer may edit or delete comments, close issues or pull requests, or
block accounts, in proportion to the conduct. As a small project, enforcement
is a judgment call by one person rather than a committee process.
